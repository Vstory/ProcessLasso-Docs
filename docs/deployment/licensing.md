# Licensing

Process Lasso is free to use. Pro features require a license after the trial period. The differences between Free and Pro, with prices, are listed on [Process Lasso Free vs Pro](https://bitsum.com/process-lasso-free-vs-pro/). Licenses are sold on the [Get Process Lasso Pro](https://bitsum.com/get-lasso-pro/) and [volume packs](https://bitsum.com/get-process-lasso-pro-volume/) pages.

## License Types

| License | Covers | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single PC | One PC per license. The quantity can be set at checkout. | Home or commercial |
| Entire Home | Up to 5 PCs in one home | Non-commercial only |
| Volume packs | 5, 25, 50 or 100 PCs per pack | Home or commercial |

You may add more than one volume pack to your cart to reach the desired quantity. We can divide or combine license packs after purchase; simply [contact us](https://bitsum.com/contact/).

## Terms

- **Monthly** and **Annual** licenses are recurring subscriptions. You can cancel at any time from the [My Licenses](https://bitsum.com/checkout/purchase-confirmation/purchase-history/) page or by [contacting us](https://bitsum.com/contact/).
- **Lifetime** licenses are a one-time payment and include all minor and major updates.
- **Entire Home** licenses are for up to 5 PCs based in a single home, for non-commercial use only. Laptops and other portable devices may travel, as long as they are based in that home. Licenses may not be shared with friends or family who don't live in the home. Commercial users should buy a [5 PC volume pack](https://bitsum.com/get-process-lasso-pro-volume/) instead.
- **New computer:** You can move a license to a new computer or Windows installation at any time by reactivating the product, as long as the number of licenses in use at once does not exceed the quantity you purchased.
- **Virtual machines** are licensed the same as physical machines.
- **Virtual desktop environments and Remote Desktop Services (RDS)** must be licensed for the maximum number of simultaneous users.
- **Commercial use** requires purchase within 30 days of deployment.
- **Refunds:** If you are not satisfied, a full refund is available up to 30 days after any payment.

The full terms are in the [End-User License Agreement](https://bitsum.com/terms-of-use/). For custom quotes or licensing questions, [contact us](https://bitsum.com/contact/).
